# Kali aesthetic with daily boot screen

The site deliberately deviates from conventional "clean minimal" portfolio styling: dark charcoal background, near-black panels, a signature green accent with warning orange/red highlights, monospace headings and a readable sans body — echoing the Kali Linux desktop. On entry, a boot animation plays once per visitor-local-day (stored as `YYYY-MM-DD` in `localStorage`, replayed when the date differs), and is skippable.

This is a real trade-off: the aesthetic is memorable and signals the owner's computer-science identity to recruiters, at the cost of conventionality. The once-per-day boot screen gives the "wow" on every fresh visit without making the animation a nuisance for returning visitors. A full desktop metaphor (draggable windows, panels) was considered and rejected — it would hide content behind gimmicks and hurt the skim-conversion goal.
