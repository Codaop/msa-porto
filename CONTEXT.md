# Portfolio

A single static site that presents who the owner is, their skills, and their past work — aimed at hiring managers, recruiters, and potential collaborators. The goal of the site is to start conversations and provide strong evidence of projects, matching visitors to the owner's current skills.

## Language

**Portfolio**:
The website as a whole — one static monolith, no backend.
_Avoid_: CV site, personal brand

**Project**:
A piece of work the owner did, presented as evidence on its own detail page with images and a description.
_Avoid_: Work, portfolio item

**Category**:
One of the three craft groupings (Software, Design, Media) that projects are filed under. Categories must not be padded with filler work.
_Avoid_: Section, tag

**Software**:
The category for programming and computer science work. UI/UX work appears only inside Software projects, never as its own category.
_Avoid_: Coding, compsci, dev

**Design**:
The merged category for graphic design and UI/UX craft evidence.
_Avoid_: Graphics, UI/UX

**Media**:
The category for photography and videography work, included because it is done as paid freelance.
_Avoid_: Multimedia, photo, video

**Experience**:
A page (not a category) covering organizations, extracurricular activities, and event volunteering — evidence of character and soft skills, not craft skill.
_Avoid_: Activities, extracurriculars, volunteer work

**Education**:
A short listing on the homepage — degrees and institutions. Deliberately thin content; no page of its own.
_Avoid_: Academics, credentials

**Boot screen**:
A one-time-per-local-day startup animation shown on site entry, replaying when the visitor's stored date differs from today, with a skip control.
_Avoid_: Loading screen, splash

**Evidence**:
The material inside a project page that proves the work happened: images, screenshots, links, and the description.
_Avoid_: Proof, deliverables

**Skill**:
A capability grouped into the site's skills section (design, development, tools & IDEs, creative, soft skills). Skills signal what the owner can do today; categories group the evidence of those skills.
_Avoid_: Ability, competency
