## The problem

A local coffee roaster was launching a web shop and needed product photos that looked consistent across the catalog and social channels.

## What I did

- Shot 20 products on a single light setup so the catalog reads as one coherent series
- Retouched in Lightroom with a shared preset, then delivered web-sized files and social crops
- Included a short shooting guide so the brand can extend the series themselves for new products

## Results

The photos shipped with the shop launch and became the brand's standard look for the season.
