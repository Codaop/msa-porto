## The problem

The festival committee wanted one visual identity that worked across A3 posters, Instagram posts, and a banner — without hiring an agency.

## What I did

- Established a grid and a recurring geometric motif that scales across all three formats
- Designed the series in Figma, then produced print-ready versions in Illustrator with correct bleed and CMYK conversion
- Provided social crops and a short style sheet so committee members could extend the identity

## Results

The series was used across all festival channels, and the committee reused the motif for next year's sponsorship deck.
