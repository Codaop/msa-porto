## The problem

Post-TBC patients need regular breathing exercises to rebuild lung capacity. Without structured guidance, however, they have little support for consistent practice or progress tracking.

## What I did

- Developed a Flutter-based mobile app to help post-TBC patients improve lung capacity, as part of the Mobile Programming Workshop
- Designed interactive breathing exercise features that guide sessions step by step
- Built daily progress tracking so patients can see their improvement over time
- Served as Mobile Developer and UI/UX Designer in a 3-person team

## Results

A working mobile app that brings breathing guidance and progress tracking into one place. It was designed and built by a 3-person team during the workshop.
