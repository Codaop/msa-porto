## The problem

The radio station wanted a short film for its anniversary — and the team had two weeks between the last broadcast and the university festival submission deadline.

## What I did

- Shot interviews and b-roll across two weeks with a pocket camera and a wireless mic
- Cut a 6-minute edit in DaVinci Resolve, including a sound pass to handle a noisy interview location
- Delivered a festival cut (festival-safe timecode and captions) and a 60-second social cut

## Results

The festival cut screened at the university festival, and the station now uses the 60-second cut for recruitment.
