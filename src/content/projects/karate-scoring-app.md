## The problem

Middle school karate coaches needed a scoring platform they could use during matches. This was a real client engagement, not a course exercise.

## What I did

- Built a web-based karate scoring platform using a Laravel monolith, with a 2-person team
- Worked requirements through feature development, including student evaluation and training progress tracking
- Owned the frontend side of the build, from interface design to interaction details

## Results

A paid product delivered to the client. This project shows how I can take a real brief from requirements through to release.
