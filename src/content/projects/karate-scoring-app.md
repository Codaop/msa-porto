## The problem

Middle school karate coaches needed a scoring platform they could actually run matches on — and the project was a real client engagement, not a course exercise.

## What I did

- Built a web-based karate scoring platform using a Laravel monolith, with a 2-person team
- Worked requirements through feature development, including student evaluation and training progress tracking
- Owned the frontend side of the build, from interface to interaction

## Results

A paid, delivered product for the client — the project where I proved I can take a real brief from requirements to release.
